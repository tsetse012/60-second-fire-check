# 60-Second Fire Check — 3-minute film, v3 (ad cut)

Direction (owner, 2026-10-29): an ad, not a walkthrough. Apple-style: grab the eye in the first second, bold
type, motion graphics and music carry it, real screens shown short and fast, no limitations section.
Generated footage = Atlas **Seedance 2.5** (budget round 1: **$30**). The v2 factual script stays as
`video/work/script-v2-factual.md` — every claim in the VO below still has to be true.

Format: 1920×1080, 24 fps, 3:00. Arc: **dark (the problem) → white (the product) → light (the door is shut)**.

Legend: **GEN** = Seedance 2.5 shot (no app UI ever generated — the product only appears in REAL shots) ·
**REAL** = our footage / live-site screen capture, sped up · **MG** = motion graphics (Remotion) ·
**♪** = music cue · **VO** = ElevenLabs narration.
